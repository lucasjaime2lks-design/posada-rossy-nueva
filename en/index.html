FAQ

Before you travel

Do rooms have a private bathroom?

No. Bathrooms are shared and the shower has hot water 24 hours, heated by gas.

Can I arrive before 15:00 or very late?

Message us your arrival time on WhatsApp. If the room is ready you can check in early, and if you arrive late, we'll wait for you.

What do rooms for 3 or more people cost?

Per night: 3 guests 165 Bs, 4 guests 200 Bs and 5 guests 250 Bs, in rooms with two and three beds. For 6 or more, ask us on WhatsApp.

Is breakfast included?

No. We don't serve meals, but you can use the shared kitchen to make your own breakfast or dinner.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp with your arrival date, nights and number of guests. We confirm availability and the final price. We're also on Booking, Airbnb, Agoda and Expedia.
